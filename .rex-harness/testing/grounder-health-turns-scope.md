# Grounder HP and base duration test scope

Source: `docs/design/weapon-combat-specification.md:159-163`. Goal: a hostile physical carrier's impact reduces magnet HP without suppressing its normal explosion; zero HP removes the device, and the base device expires after two opponent turn completions. This is one part of weapon 28; radius/core upgrades, visual, and negative carrier tests remain in the accepted full scope `.rex-harness/testing/grounder-magnet-scope.md`. No generic mine or force-field changes.

Public seams: `BattleSimulation.TryFire`, `Spawn`, `Tick`, turn transition, `GrounderDeployed`, new `GrounderDamaged`, and `GrounderRemoved` events. Map acceptance to assertions: one 24-damage shot reports remaining HP 21 from the prototype's 45, another destroys it and emits removal once; `ProjectileExploded` fires for both; after placement the device remains through the first opposing turn completion and is removed on the second. No direct private-field assertions. The smallest independent failing slice is damage event after one shot; this would fail before implementation while the attraction test stays green. Complete only when focused Unity tests pass. Never skip or weaken assertions to get green.
