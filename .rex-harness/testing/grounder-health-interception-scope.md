# Grounder health interception scope

User goal: a hostile physical projectile attracted to the grounder explodes at the device and reduces its HP; two ordinary 24-damage hits destroy the current 45-HP prototype. Non-goals: player HP balancing, radius/core upgrades, energy/friendly classification, visual skin, and other weapon changes. Public test seam: `BattleSimulation.TryFire`, `Spawn`, `Tick`, `GrounderDeployed`, `GrounderRemoved`, and `ProjectileExploded`. Acceptance: after the first incoming shot, one explosion and no device removal; after the second, a second explosion and exactly one removal. The smallest independently failing slice is two shots through this public simulation seam. Do not inspect private health or weaken/remove the assertions. Passing targeted Unity EditMode test is completion evidence for this slice.
